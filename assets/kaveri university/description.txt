🚀 **Empowering 200+ Students with Data Structures & Algorithms using C!**

I’m happy to share my experience of conducting a **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA) training session using C Programming** at **Kaveri University**.

👨‍💻 **Audience:** 200+ Students  
📚 **Focus:** Data Structures & Algorithms using C  
🎯 **Objective:** Strengthening programming fundamentals, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills for technical careers and placements.

During the training, we explored how fundamental data structures and algorithms are implemented in C and, more importantly, how to approach programming problems logically.

Some of the key areas covered included:

🔹 Arrays & Strings  
🔹 Linked Lists  
🔹 Stacks & Queues  
🔹 Searching & Sorting  
🔹 Time & Space Complexity  
🔹 Recursion  
🔹 Problem-Solving & Algorithmic Thinking  
🔹 Practical coding and implementation

What made the session particularly exciting was the **energy and participation of 200+ students**. Interacting with such a large group of aspiring engineers and helping them strengthen their DSA fundamentals was a great learning experience for me as a trainer as well.

💡 **DSA is not just about writing code — it's about learning how to think, optimize, and solve problems efficiently.**
